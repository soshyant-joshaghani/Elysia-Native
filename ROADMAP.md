# Roadmap

Elysia-Native is the starter-tier Elysia kit with a frontend slot. It stays aligned with the folder contract and the wire contract in [foxg-kit](../../../README.md).

- Keep `apps/sample`, `base`, and `system` in step with Fast-Svelte and Elysia-Svelte
- Keep the wire contract in [CONTRACT.md](../../../CONTRACT.md) identical
- Keep the Python `__ctrl__` command surface (dev, test, prod, flatten, remote, web)
- Native shells stay reserved until the API in this family is the one you ship
